6/42 Lotto Tracker 3D

PCSO results. Pick a year, then a month, then a day.

Straight means the same digit in the same position. Last digits counts from the right (a 4 straight = your last 4 digits equal the draw's last 4), First digits from the left, and Any straight stretch takes the longest run of neighbouring positions that match.

to

Type any amount of numbers (1 to 58, spaces or commas). Every draw is checked for how many of its 6 numbers are in your list, in any order and any place. Click a count to show only those draws. The table compares 6/42, 6/55 and 6/58 side by side for the same month and year. A number above a game's top number (like 48 on 6/42) cannot come out there.

to

Looks at every draw of the chosen month in the years you pick, then builds combinations of 6 numbers from the numbers that came out, favouring the ones that came out more often. This is a fun way to pick numbers from history, not a prediction: every draw is random and no combination is more likely to win.

to

Compares the draws of that month across the years you pick. Numbers can be in any order and any place. Each group lists the draws that have the same 3, 4, 5 or more numbers in common (highlighted).

Looks at every saved draw of this game and ranks the numbers that came out in the same draw as yours most often. This is a history count, not a guarantee: every draw is random.
